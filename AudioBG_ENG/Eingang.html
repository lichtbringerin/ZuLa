    Station 1 – Entrance

    Textversion

    Welcome to the Botanical Garden of the University of Wuerzburg! Our Botanical Garden has a long history. It was founded in 1696. Originally, it was located behind the Juliusspital hospital in the centre of Wuerzburg. On March 16th 1945, most parts of the garden were destroyed, which is why it was finally moved to its current location here on the Dallenberg in 1960. Since then, the garden has been continuously developed. The garden is part of the University of Wuerzburg and covers an area of around 9 hectares with outdoor areas and several greenhouses. Around 9,000 plant species are cultivated on its grounds. It serves scientific teaching, research, the public, environmental education and as a place of learning for schools. We hope you enjoy using this audio guide to embark on a journey through the world of plants. As you make your way through the garden, you will come across 16 stations on different topics that highlight different aspects of the plant world. Here you will discover areas with native, regional flora, Mediterranean vegetation and tropical crops. The stations are not arranged in a fixed order, so you can customise your tour to suit your individual preferences. Please follow the garden rules and do not leave the designated paths or damage the plants. If you have any further questions, please feel free to ask the staff at any time. We wish you a pleasant time at the Wuerzburg Botanical Garden and hope you enjoy your journey through the fascinating world of plants.
