    Station 6 – Bees

    Textversion

    Bees are among the most important pollinators in nature and therefore play a central role here in the Botanical Garden. When we talk about bees, many people immediately think of honey bees. But they are only one of many species. There are around 600 different species of wild bees in Germany. Here in the Wuerzburg Botanical Garden, almost 100 species of these pollinators were counted as part of a thesis. Unlike honey bees, wild bees usually live solitarily as loners. They do not form large colonies and do not produce honey. But their tasks are just as important. They pollinate flowers and thus ensure the reproduction of many plants. This way, they contribute significantly to the preservation of biodiversity. Without bees, many of our native plants would not exist, and numerous types of fruit and vegetables, such as those found in vegetable patches or cottage gardens, would be virtually inconceivable without their pollination. Find out more about the fascinating world of bees in the pavilion and take a look at the display case with a colony of honey bees. Can you spot the queen bee? If you want to see some wild bees, you can find a so-called bee hotel in the cottage garden.
