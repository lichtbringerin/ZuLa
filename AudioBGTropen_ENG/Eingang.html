    Station 1 – Entrance Tropical Glasshouse

    Textversion

    Welcome to the Tropical House at the Würzburg Botanical Garden. This section of your journey through the Earth’s vegetation zones takes you through various habitats of the tropics and subtropics. Here, you will encounter more than just the classic tropical rainforest with its constant humidity. The tropics, located within the tropics of cancer and capricorn, encompass a wide variety of habitats that can differ significantly in temperature, precipitation, humidity, and vegetation. On the vegetation map opposite this station, you can get an overview of the tropical vegetation zones around the equator between the two tropics on the various continents. On your tour, you will therefore pass through several greenhouse areas: You begin in the ever-humid lowland rainforest and proceed from there to the cooler mountain cloud forest. Your journey then takes you to dry, arid habitats of the tropics, before you discover the diversity of flowers in the Flower Gallery and, at the end, learn about tropical crops. Follow the tour and embark on a journey through the fascinating world of tropical plants, and feel free to use the audio and video stations along the way.
