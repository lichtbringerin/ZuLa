    Station 5 – Mountain Cloud Forrest

    Textversion

    In the next section of your journey, you’ll leave the lowland rainforest and enter a significantly cooler climate zone: the mountain cloud forest. These forests are located at elevations between about 1,000 and 3,000 meters. Here, moist air masses encounter the mountains, rise, and cool. As this happens, the water in the air condenses, forming dense clouds and fog that often shroud the forest permanently. The air here is almost constantly saturated with moisture. Even when it isn’t raining, the fog and clouds ensure that there is plenty of water available. As a result, annual precipitation levels are even higher than in the lowland rainforest. At the same time, temperatures are significantly lower, ranging from about 12 to 18 degrees. These climatic conditions also affect the vegetation. The trees remain smaller and are less densely packed, allowing more light to reach the forest floor. In contrast to the lowland rainforest, the forest floor here is much more densely vegetated. Many ferns, mosses, and herbaceous plants form a dense, evergreen layer. The branches of the trees are home to numerous epiphytes such as bromeliads, orchids, and mosses, which thrive in the high humidity. You may have already noticed that there are hardly any palm trees growing here. Instead, tree ferns dominate the landscape. These plants are not seed plants but vascular spore plants and have a very long evolutionary history. That is why they are referred to as living fossils. When you walk beneath a tree fern, take a moment to look at the underside of its fronds. There you will see small brown dots, known as sporangia. This is where the numerous spores are formed and released for reproduction. Mountain cloud forests are among the most sensitive and threatened habitats on Earth, as many monoculture plantations have replaced them. Many of the species found here are endemic, meaning they occur only in very limited regions and are particularly sensitive to changes in their habitat. Take your time on the tour and pay attention to the dense vegetation and the humid, almost misty atmosphere.
