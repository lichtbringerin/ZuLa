    Station 10 – Crop Plants 2

    Textversion

    In the final section of your journey, you’ll encounter tropical crops that are primarily used as food. Many of these are familiar to us from everyday life, even if they do not grow in our region. These include, for example, bananas, pineapples, cocoa, and various spices such as vanilla and pepper. Staple foods like rice and sugarcane also come from tropical regions and play an important role worldwide. One example of a food crop is the coffee plant. The seeds of the coffee cherry, known as coffee beans, are processed and roasted after harvest. Coffee is now one of the most widely consumed beverages in the world. The average annual per capita coffee consumption in Germany is over 160 liters. Coffea arabica originally comes from the highlands of Ethiopia and Sudan, but is now cultivated in many tropical and subtropical regions. However, the large-scale cultivation of such plants also has an impact on the environment and society. Large areas are often cleared for plantations, resulting in the loss of species-rich tropical habitats. At the same time, monocultures create ecological problems, and working conditions are also difficult in many growing regions. Another particularly striking example is the cacao tree. Its fruits grow directly on the trunk—a phenomenon known as cauliflory. The pods contain the beans from which cacao is extracted. After harvesting, they are fermented, dried, and processed, serving, for example, as the basis for chocolate. If you look around further, you’ll discover many other plants that shape our daily lives, even though they come from distant regions. This concludes your tour of the Tropical House at the Würzburg Botanical Garden. You have explored various tropical habitats, from the perpetually humid rainforest to arid regions and diverse crop plants. Perhaps you’ve noticed some similarities as well as differences compared to our forests and plants. Plants are constantly developing new strategies to adapt to their environment, whether in terms of water, light, or nutrients.
